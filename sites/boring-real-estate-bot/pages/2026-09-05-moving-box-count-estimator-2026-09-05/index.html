Moving Box Count Estimator Update

People underestimate box count and end up buying expensive emergency boxes the night before moving.

Who searches this?

Renters planning a local move and comparing mover quotes.

Useful checklist

  1. Count closets first; they usually drive the surprise box total.
  2. Use small boxes for books, dishes, tools, and pantry cans.
  3. Use medium boxes for most household goods; avoid overfilling large boxes.
  4. Reserve wardrobe boxes for hanging clothes only if wrinkle-free delivery matters.
  5. Buy tape, labels, and a marker pack before packing day starts.

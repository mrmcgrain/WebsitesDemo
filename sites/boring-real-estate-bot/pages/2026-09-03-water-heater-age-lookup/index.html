Water Heater Age Lookup Checklist

Homeowners see a rusty tank and need to know whether it is near end-of-life before it leaks.

Who searches this?

Homeowners, landlords, and buyers reviewing inspection notes.

Useful checklist

  1. Photograph the model and serial-number sticker before searching online.
  2. Find the manufacturer date code; brand formats differ.
  3. Check for water in the drain pan, rust trails, or damp flooring nearby.
  4. Ask for replacement quotes before failure if the tank is 10+ years old.
  5. Note whether the unit is gas, electric, tankless, garage, closet, or attic mounted.

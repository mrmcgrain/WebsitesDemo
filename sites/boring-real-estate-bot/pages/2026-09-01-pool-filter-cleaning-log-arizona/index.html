Useful checklist

  1. Record clean-filter PSI after a full backwash or cartridge cleaning.
  2. Clean again when pressure rises 8-10 PSI over the baseline.
  3. After a dust storm, skim first, then check PSI before running the pump longer.
  4. Inspect cartridges for cracked bands, flattened pleats, or brittle end caps.
  5. Keep a dated note of cleanings so pool techs can spot recurring problems.

Short-Term Rental Restock List

Hosts lose reviews over boring missing items: batteries, paper towels, coffee filters, and trash bags.

Who searches this?

Small short-term rental hosts and cleaners doing same-day turnovers.

Useful checklist

  1. Create minimum par levels for paper towels, toilet paper, trash bags, and soap.
  2. Keep batteries for remotes, keypad locks, smoke detectors, and fans.
  3. Photograph supply closets after every turnover for accountability.
  4. Separate guest-facing extras from cleaner-only backup inventory.
  5. Review restock receipts monthly to catch waste or underpricing.

Useful checklist

  1. Take one wide photo from the street before doing any work.
  2. Photograph each cited area separately with the notice number visible nearby.
  3. Save receipts for gravel, weed treatment, haul-away, or landscaping labor.
  4. Take matching after photos at the same angle and time of day if possible.
  5. Email the HOA a short note with photos attached before the cure-date deadline.

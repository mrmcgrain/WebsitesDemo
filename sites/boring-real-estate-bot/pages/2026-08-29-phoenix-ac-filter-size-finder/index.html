Phoenix AC Filter Size Finder

People forget HVAC filter dimensions, then buy the wrong 3-pack during the first 110° week.

Who searches this?

Phoenix renters, homeowners, and property managers doing routine AC maintenance.

Useful checklist

  1. Open the return grille and photograph the printed dimensions on the old filter.
  2. If the label is unreadable, measure length, width, and depth with a tape measure.
  3. Write the size on the inside of the utility closet door.
  4. Set a 45-day reminder during monsoon/dust season; 60-90 days is fine in cooler months.
  5. Buy MERV 8-11 for most residential systems unless the HVAC tech specifies otherwise.
